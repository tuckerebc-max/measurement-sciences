## `OPT-67-MEASUREMENT` — A score is not the construct

**Learner target:** Build a construct and evidence map before recommending a score use.

**Fictional assessment:** A short written-response measure is highly consistent across raters but samples only one narrow form of academic language. A district proposes using the score to assign students to an advanced research pathway.

**Learner task:** Define the intended construct and decision. Identify evidence that supports precision, evidence that is missing for validity, possible construct underrepresentation, accessibility questions, subgroup questions, and consequences. Recommend a bounded next step or nonrecommendation.

**Required artifact:** construct map, intended-use statement, validity evidence table, fairness / access review, residual-risk memo.

**Nonmodel:** “The reliability coefficient proves the assessment is valid.”  
**Feedback move:** Ask “What interpretation is being made, for whom, and what evidence would justify that interpretation?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent organizes evidence and proposes missing checks; measurement expert owns the interpretation and decision boundary.

**Exit test:** Provide a second measure with lower precision but broader construct coverage. Learner must compare evidence rather than rank by one statistic.
