# `OPT-67-MEASUREMENT` textbook-skill architecture

**Architecture ID:** `F2-ARCH-67-MEASUREMENT-001`  
**Status:** `SPEC_DRAFT — COMPOSITE`  
**Promise:** Build advanced measurement judgment: define constructs, assemble evidence, interpret uncertainty, examine consequences, and improve evaluators.

## Learner change

Before: the learner treats a score, model, or statistic as the construct.  
After: the learner states the intended interpretation, designs an evidence argument, tests quality and fairness, communicates limits, and monitors consequences.

## Progression

`Define → Design → Measure → Analyze → Validate → Interpret → Report → Improve`

## Unit map

| Unit | Work | Learner evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Constructs, uses, populations, and consequences | construct map and decision brief |
| 2 | Design, measures, data, and models | design / measurement memo |
| 3 | Validity, reliability, and uncertainty | evidence argument and precision audit |
| 4 | Fairness, accessibility, and subgroup evidence | fairness / access review |
| 5 | Interpretation, reporting, and review | technical report and critique response |
| 6 | Evaluator and agent improvement | audit, revision, rerun, and residual-risk memo |

## Field / program boundary

Field-level standards supply quality dimensions. The UMD QMMS program supplies one labeled doctoral exemplar. Project synthesis must remain visible in every competency record.

## Human-plus-agent design

The agent may calculate, compare, annotate, and generate candidate evidence maps. The human defines the construct, adjudicates validity and fairness, owns the decision rule, and determines whether evidence is sufficient.

## Evaluator seed

Dimensions: construct clarity, design fit, evidence sufficiency, technical quality, uncertainty, fairness / accessibility, consequence awareness, reporting transparency, and revision discipline.

## First drafting packet

Draft `MEAS-001` will critique a fictional assessment claim, build a construct / evidence map, identify missing validity evidence, and specify an evaluator revision. It will not imply a universal doctoral curriculum.
